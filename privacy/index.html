Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kaelix Cyber Inc (“Kaelix,” “we,” or “us”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data in connection with our business, our website at kaelix.io, and our software and services, including Kaelix Vantage. Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact details in Section 12.

1Whose Personal Data We Collect

  • Customer personnel: People who work for our customers, including purchasing, billing, and administrative contacts and people who contact us for support.
  • Prospects, partners, and vendors: People at organizations we are discussing business with, and partners, suppliers, and vendors we work with.
  • Website visitors: kaelix.io does not use contact forms, newsletter sign-ups, analytics, or tracking cookies, and we do not collect personal data from website visits.

2What Personal Data We Collect

  • Business contact information: Name, job title, organization, business email address, telephone number, and business mailing address.
  • Contract and billing information: Contract, order, invoice, and payment information for the organization and its contacts.
  • Support information: Information in support requests and correspondence, and information we observe in a customer’s environment while providing support at the customer’s request.
  • License activation information: For internet-connected deployments of our software, the information needed to activate the license.
  • Customer exports: Exports of customer content that a customer chooses to send to us under its license agreement.

3Our Software

Kaelix Vantage runs in the customer’s own environment. It contains no analytics or usage telemetry and does not send usage data or customer content to Kaelix. Personal data stored in a customer’s deployment is controlled by that customer.

We receive personal data from a customer’s deployment only when the customer provides it: through support access the customer grants, exports the customer sends us, or license activation for connected deployments. When we handle personal data on a customer’s behalf, we act on the customer’s instructions and under our agreement with the customer.

If a customer configures the software to use a third-party AI model provider, content processed by the AI features is sent from the customer’s environment to that provider under the provider’s terms. That connection is controlled by the customer, not by Kaelix.

4How We Use Personal Data

  • To provide, deliver, license, support, and maintain our software and services.
  • To prepare and manage quotes, orders, contracts, invoices, and payments.
  • To communicate with customers, partners, and vendors about our business relationship.
  • To protect the security of our systems and our customers’ data, and to investigate incidents.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, tax, export-control, and contractual obligations, and to establish or defend legal claims.

We do not sell personal data, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising.

5How We Disclose Personal Data

  • Service providers: Companies that provide services to us, such as email, collaboration, document storage, accounting, and payment processing, under obligations to protect the data and use it only to provide services to us.
  • Professional advisors: Attorneys, accountants, auditors, and insurers, under duties of confidentiality.
  • The customer you work for: Other people within the same customer organization, as needed to manage the customer relationship.
  • Legal and safety: Where required by law, legal process, or government request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Kaelix, our customers, or others.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to this policy.

6Data Retention

We keep personal data for as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, including the length of the customer relationship, and afterward as needed to meet legal, tax, and contractual requirements and to resolve disputes. We do not keep credentials issued for a support session beyond that session. When personal data is no longer needed, we delete it or de-identify it.

7Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal data and limit access to people who need it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. If we confirm unauthorized access to or disclosure of a customer’s data in our possession, we notify the customer as stated in our agreements, and we notify individuals and authorities where the law requires.

8Where Data Is Processed

Kaelix is based in the United States, and we process personal data in the United States. If you provide personal data to us from outside the United States, it will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

9Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, including under the privacy laws of Utah and other U.S. states, you may have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we process your personal data and get access to a copy of it.
  • Correct inaccurate personal data.
  • Delete personal data you provided to us or that we hold about you.
  • Opt out of the sale of personal data, targeted advertising, or profiling. We do not engage in these activities.

We honor these requests from any individual, wherever located, to the extent we can, subject to legal exceptions. To make a request, email privacy@kaelix.io. We will verify your identity before acting on a request and respond within the time required by applicable law. You may use an authorized agent with your signed permission. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision, and we will explain the outcome of the appeal in writing.

Where we handle personal data on behalf of a customer, as described in Section 3, we will refer your request to that customer and assist the customer in responding.

10Children

Our products and services are intended for organizations and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

11Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will post the updated policy with a new effective date and, where the changes are material, notify customers by email or within our software.

12Contact Us

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email privacy@kaelix.io.

For anything else, reach us through our Contact Us page.
